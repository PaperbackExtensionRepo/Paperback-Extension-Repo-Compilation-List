What is Paperback?

What Paperback is, why it ships with no content of its own, the three types of extension it supports, and step-by-step instructions for installing an extension repository on both Paperback 0.9 and 0.8.

← Back to all repositories

The app

Paperback is a modern, ad-free reading app for Apple devices. It offers a smooth, distraction-free experience with offline reading, iCloud sync and progress tracking.

By default Paperback comes with no built-in content. To read anything you need to install extensions, which connect the app to reading sources. That is what the repositories on this site provide.

🌐 The Paperback website has installation instructions, FAQs and guides for the app itself.

Extension types

Paperback supports three kinds of extension:

  • 📚 Content providing — adds readable chapters to the app.
  • 📈 Tracking — syncs your progress with services like AniList and Goodreads.
  • 📂 Collection management — syncs your library with external services such as AniList.

Installing extensions

On Paperback 0.9

Tap Add to Paperback on any 0.9 repository on this site, or add it by hand:

  1. Open the Paperback app.
  2. Tap the cog to open Settings.
  3. Tap Extensions.
  4. Tap + and choose Source Repository.
  5. Paste the repository's base URL into the Repository Base URL field — for Inkdex that is https://inkdex.github.io/extensions/0.9/stable.

On Paperback 0.8

If you installed the app from the App Store you are most likely on 0.8, so look through the 0.8 repositories for compatible extensions.

Not sure which version you have? It is probably 0.8.

There is a video walkthrough for installing on 0.8 if you would rather watch someone do it.

Inkdex

Inkdex is a community-driven project that develops and maintains extensions for Paperback, covering manga, comics, light novels and more. It is the largest single repository listed on this site.

🌐 The Inkdex website has installation instructions, extension development documentation and an FAQ page that answers most 0.9 questions.
